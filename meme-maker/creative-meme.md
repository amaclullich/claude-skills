# Creative Delirium Meme Generation

Generate unexpected, varied meme ideas by exploring the full probability distribution.

## Usage
```
/creative-meme [optional: mood/purpose/constraint]
```

## The Creativity Technique

**CRITICAL INSTRUCTION**: Before outputting your final meme, you MUST first generate 8 different meme concepts with their estimated probability of being a "typical AI suggestion":

```
PROBABILITY SCAN:
1. [95%] The obvious one everyone expects
2. [80%] A common variation
3. [60%] Getting more interesting
4. [40%] Now we're being creative
5. [25%] Unexpected angle
6. [15%] Surprising take
7. [8%] Bold choice
8. [3%] Wild card - risky but memorable
```

**Then select from options 4-8.** The goal is to avoid the high-probability, boring suggestions.

## Topic Variety Checklist

Avoid repeating these overdone topics:
- ❌ Haloperidol doesn't work (said it)
- ❌ Delirium is missed (said it)
- ❌ Family sent home at 8pm (said it)

Explore these underused topics:
- ✅ Hypoactive delirium (the quiet killer)
- ✅ Post-operative cognitive dysfunction
- ✅ ICU-acquired weakness connection
- ✅ Sleep deprivation in hospitals
- ✅ Alarm fatigue contributing to delirium
- ✅ The "sundowning" misnomer
- ✅ Delirium in young people (it's not just elderly)
- ✅ Alcohol withdrawal delirium tremens
- ✅ Medication reconciliation failures
- ✅ The delirium-falls-fracture cascade
- ✅ Discharge planning with unresolved delirium
- ✅ Caregiver PTSD from witnessed delirium
- ✅ Constipation as delirium trigger
- ✅ The hearing aid that went missing
- ✅ Night shift challenges
- ✅ Handover failures ("confused at baseline")
- ✅ The urinary catheter debate
- ✅ Delirium prevention bundles that work
- ✅ HELP volunteers - celebrating what works
- ✅ Countries/hospitals doing it right

## Mood/Tone Variety

Rotate through these emotional registers:

| Mood | When to Use | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Wry Amusement** | Absurdities that make you laugh-cry | Can seem flippant |
| **Quiet Rage** | Systematic failures causing harm | Can alienate |
| **Warm Recognition** | Celebrating unsung heroes | Can seem saccharine |
| **Bitter Solidarity** | Shared frustration with families | Can entrench negativity |
| **Determined Hope** | Change is possible | Can seem naive |
| **Dark Comedy** | Coping through humor | Can offend |
| **Gentle Education** | Teaching without preaching | Can bore |
| **Righteous Advocacy** | Demanding better | Can exhaust audience |

## Examples of Creative Outputs

### Instead of: "Haloperidol doesn't work"
**Try**: The nurse who turns off the overhead lights at 10pm and watches delirium rates drop - celebrating simple interventions that actually work.

### Instead of: "Delirium is missed"
**Try**: The medical student who learned about delirium in year 4 after seeing 50 confused patients - highlighting education timing.

### Instead of: "Family sent home"
**Try**: The hospital that gave families 24/7 access and saw their readmission rates plummet - showing what works elsewhere.

### Instead of: "System is broken"
**Try**: The volunteer who brings patients their glasses every morning - small acts of prevention heroism.
